## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is an editor's proof of the visitor's own task: a plain typed request, marked in red pencil until it says what was meant. It refuses the category default of a centred headline over a grid of eight identical feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Paper ground #F4F2EC, near-black #141414 type, one red-orange #F04E23 reserved for pencil marks and the copy action. A typewriter-like monospace for typed text and headings, a plain sans for body. Components look like a typed sheet, margin notes, struck words, carets and numbered editorial entries; no cards, no icons.

STORY: The visitor sees their kind of request improved in place, understands that each skill does one such edit, reads the eight as a numbered style sheet, sees that every line was tested and that losses are published, and copies the install command.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left half: logo and nav row, a two-line monospace headline, a two-line sans subhead, the install command field with an orange Copy button. Right third: a tilted typed sheet with the original task and the /deeper rewrite carrying red pencil marks and margin notes, a pencil lying at its right edge. The primary action is the Copy button under the subhead.
